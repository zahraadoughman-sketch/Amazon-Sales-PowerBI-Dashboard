# Amazon Sales & Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon multi-year sales, product categories, and customer review metrics to deliver clear business insights.

## Files Included
* AmzonSalesCV.pbix: The interactive Power BI dashboard featuring custom data modeling, DAX measures, and a professional executive UI.
* Amazon_Combined_Data.xlsx: The raw transactional dataset used for sales and category analysis.

## Key Dashboard Features
* KPI Cards: Total YTD Sales ($2.18M), QTD Sales ($811.09K), YTD Products Sold (28K), and YTD Reviews (19M).
* Trend Analysis: Monthly performance tracking and weekly distribution charts highlighting key sales peaks.
* Category Breakdown: Product category matrix displaying YTD sales, QTD sales, and percentage contributions (%GT YTD Sales).
* Product Rankings: Top 5 products by YTD sales and Top 5 products by YTD reviews.
* Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering by Product Category and Quarters.

## Key Insights
* **Dominant Revenue Category:** Men Shoes stands out as the primary revenue driver, single-handedly accounting for 43.18% of total store sales ($940,266).
* **Strong Q4 Seasonality:** Weekly trend analysis highlights a massive performance surge during the final quarter, peaking dramatically around Week 50 with nearly $398K in a single week.
* **Secondary Categories Performance:** Camera and Men Clothes follow as the next significant revenue contributors, holding 22.62% ($492.52K) and 16.42% ($357.64K) of total sales respectively.
* **Customer Engagement:** High customer interest is reflected in the product reviews ranking, with top items such as SanDisk microSD cards driving substantial consumer engagement volumes.
